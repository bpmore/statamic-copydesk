## No warranty, and specifically no verdict

The capitals are below, as usual. This part is in plain words because it is the
part people actually need.

This Software grades text with readability formulas that were calibrated on
English samples decades ago, and shows the result as a band because the
formulas are not precise enough for a decimal. A grade is a heuristic. Nothing
this Software produces is a finding of conformance with any standard, a
determination under WCAG success criterion 3.1.5 or any other, or a judgement
about whether a particular person can read a particular page. It never says a
page fails WCAG, and that is deliberate. A rule it fires is worth looking at; a
page it fires nothing on has not been shown to be plain.
