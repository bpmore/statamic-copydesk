## news-style-starter

A few conventions most newsrooms share, for numbers, times and dates, described in Copydesk's own words. A starting point, not any stylebook: a house that follows a licensed stylebook writes its rules in its own pack, and can turn any of these off.

### `news-no-numeral-start` (sentence, tip)

Message: Spell the number out, or reword so the sentence starts with a word.

- [ ] Check: starts_with_numeral

### `news-percent-word` (pattern, tip)

Message: In running text, write the word "percent" after the number, as in 5 percent.

- [ ] Pattern: `\d(?:[.,]\d+)?\s?%`

### `news-no-on-the-hour` (pattern, tip)

Message: On the hour, the minutes add nothing. Write 9 a.m., not 9:00 a.m.

- [ ] Pattern: `\b(?:1[0-2]|[1-9]):00\s?(?:a\.m\.|p\.m\.|[aApP]\.?[mM]\.?)(?![\p{L}])`

### `news-am-pm` (pattern, tip)

Message: Write a.m. and p.m., lowercase with periods.

- [ ] Pattern: `\b(?:1[0-2]|[1-9])(?::[0-5]\d)?\s?(?:AM|PM|am|pm|A\.M\.|P\.M\.)(?![\p{L}])`

### `news-noon-midnight` (pattern, tip)

Message: Say noon or midnight; 12 a.m. and 12 p.m. are easy to confuse.

- [ ] Pattern: `\b12(?::00)?\s?(?:p\.m\.|a\.m\.|[aApP]\.?[mM]\.?)(?![\p{L}])`

### `news-no-date-ordinal` (pattern, tip)

Message: In a date, the number needs no "st", "nd", "rd" or "th". Write March 3, not March 3rd.

- [ ] Pattern: `\b(?:Jan(?:uary)?|Feb(?:ruary)?|Mar(?:ch)?|Apr(?:il)?|May|June?|July?|Aug(?:ust)?|Sept?(?:ember)?|Oct(?:ober)?|Nov(?:ember)?|Dec(?:ember)?)\.?\s+\d{1,2}(?:st|nd|rd|th)\b`
